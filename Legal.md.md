The objective is to preserve Joey Soprano's ownership and commercial control while allowing properly attributed, noncommercial collaboration.

Before the license, two important legal distinctions need to be established:

1. Ownership cannot be created merely by declaring it. Under U.S. copyright law, source code, original documentation, and other qualifying creative expression can be protected.
